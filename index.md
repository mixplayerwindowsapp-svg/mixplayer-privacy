# MixPlayer privacy policy

_Last updated: 21 November 2026_

MixPlayer is a music player that runs entirely on your PC.

* **No data collection.** MixPlayer does not collect, store, send or share any
  personal information. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
* **No internet use.** MixPlayer does not connect to the internet.
* **Your files stay on your PC.** MixPlayer only opens the audio files and playlists
  you choose. Your playlist, volume, equaliser and display settings are saved on
  your own PC so they are remembered next time, and are removed when you uninstall
  the app.

If you have questions about this policy, contact: mixplayerwindowsapp@gmail.com
